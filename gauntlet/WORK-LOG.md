# Material Work Log

Record major phases, prototypes, rebuilds, dead ends, and pivots. This is not a diary and should not duplicate routine git history.

| ID | Phase | Objective | Action/prototype | Result | Evidence produced | Beliefs/constraints affected | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Major rebuilds

For each substantial rebuild, record:
- what forced it;
- which upstream assumption/constraint failed;
- what work was invalidated;
- what work remained valid;
- whether earlier intelligence, critique, or testing plausibly could have prevented the cost.
