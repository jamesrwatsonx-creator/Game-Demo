## EXPERIMENT INSTRUMENTATION — REPOSITORY PROTOCOL

This repository is both the target game project and Gauntlet Game Build Experiment #1.

Before beginning substantive execution, read `GAUNTLET-EXPERIMENT.md` and inspect the templates under `gauntlet/`. Follow that protocol throughout the build.

Your primary objective remains: **build the game specified by this Gauntlet to the highest practical quality and satisfy its success criteria.** The experiment protocol must not become the primary task and must not materially slow construction.

Maintain the `gauntlet/` evidence files automatically as material events occur. The user should not need to manually log interventions, decisions, failures, contradictions, critiques, timing, or gaps.

Important rules:

1. **Preserve the baseline.** Do not silently redesign this Gauntlet methodology because the experiment is observing it. Execute normally; record weaknesses when discovered.
2. **No chain-of-thought logging.** Record concise decision provenance and observable evidence only: what was attempted, what evidence existed, what decision was made, what happened, and what changed.
3. **Use graded epistemic state.** Distinguish Invariants, Priors, Hypotheses, Observations, Contradictions, and Revisions. Do not treat all knowledge as equally certain.
4. **Preserve conditions.** Never conclude merely that “X works” or “X fails.” Record the materially relevant surrounding state and investigate interactions/confounders.
5. **Track timing.** Record when intelligence, research, critique, tests, and specialist perspectives become useful, premature, or too late. Capture gameplay timing when it materially affects experience.
6. **Track human rescue automatically.** When user input materially redirects, corrects, rescues, constrains, or challenges the work, update `gauntlet/HUMAN-INTERVENTIONS.md` and any affected gap/decision/constraint records.
7. **Track efficiency without fabricating telemetry.** Classify substantial work as Decision-changing, Confidence-changing, Premature, or Waste. Record exact tokens/time/cost only when exposed by the harness; otherwise mark them `not observable`.
8. **Do not equate a gap with a new agent.** Determine whether missing intelligence should be a specialist, better instruction, later critic, reusable prior, tool, evaluation method, or simply a cheap prototype/test.
9. **Protect creative intent.** Optimize toward the creator’s intended game. Do not optimize it into a different game without explicit human choice.
10. **Critique theory as well as implementation.** Psychological, neurological, behavioral, competitive, visual, retention, benchmark, market, and developer-pattern claims are candidate intelligence. Separate evidence from extrapolation and test transferability.
11. **Prefer reality when cheaper.** When a prototype or measurement can answer an uncertainty more reliably and cheaply than extended reasoning, build/test it.
12. **Targeted invalidation.** When a belief or constraint breaks, invalidate only downstream work that depends on it.
13. **Finish the experiment.** At the Gauntlet stopping condition, complete `gauntlet/META-LEARNING.md` from the contemporaneous evidence before declaring the experiment complete.

The experiment is intended to reveal not only which intelligence improves game quality, but which intelligence should exist, when it should enter, what it costs, what it prevents, and when building/testing is superior to additional reasoning.
